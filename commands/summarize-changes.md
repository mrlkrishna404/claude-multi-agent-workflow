# Summarize Changes

Summarize the current changes in this repository in a concise, readable way.

Requirements:
- Review the current diff and relevant changed files.
- Identify the main purpose of the change and the key files involved.
- Explain what changed at a high level and why it matters.
- Call out any risks, assumptions, or follow-up items.
- Keep the output brief but actionable.

Format:
- Title: brief summary of the work
- What changed
- Why it changed
- Impact / risk
- Validation or next steps

If the diff is small, include file-level details. If the diff is large, focus on the major themes and the most important files.
